Published Books

Transitory Gardens, Uprooted Lives

Yale University Press, 1993.

This book of images and text by Margaret Morton and Diana Balmori looks at gardens built by unhoused individuals, who appropriated space on empty lots and behind squatted buildings to make sites of temporary respite.

148 pages, 112 photographs.

The Tunnel

Yale University Press, 1995.
Published in German translation as Der Tunnel.

The Tunnel examines the lives of some of New York City's unhoused citizens who, in the early 1990s, built homes in an Amtrak tunnel under the West Side Highway.

xx pages, xx photographs.

Fragile Dwelling

Aperture Foundation, 2000.

Fragile Dwelling attends to the ingenuity with which unhoused people in New York City created not only places to live but also communities that offered a sense of pride, place, and individuality.

xx pages, xx photographs.

Glass House

Penn State University Press, 2004.

The photographs and oral histories in Glass House document the resourcefulness of thirty-five squatters, who entered a derelict glass factory on New York City’s Lower East Side determined to rehabilitate it into permanent housing.

xx pages, xx photographs.

Cities of the Dead: The Ancestral Cemeteries of Kyrgyzstan

University of Washington Press, 2014.

Cities of the Dead consists of photographs of Kyrgyz ancestral cemeteries seen across several Kyrgyz provinces, architectural amalgams of animist motifs and Islamic iconography, carrying the residues of Kyrgyzstan’s Soviet past.

xx pages, xx photographs.

The Farley

2012

The Farley is a portrait of the sorting rooms, service windows, observation decks, and offices in the 1.4 million square-foot Farley Post Office (constructed 1914), which were gutted for conversion into Moynihan Train Station.

xx photographs.
