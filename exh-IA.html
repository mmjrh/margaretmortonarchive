Through Padlocks, Behind Barricades

Interference Archive, October 2025 - January 2026

These programs accompanied the exhibition:

  • November 11, 2025: Artists on Squatting: Fly, Ash Thayer, and Seth Tobocman (Moderated by Amy Starecheski)
  • November 18, 2025: Archives, Displacement, and the Struggle for Housing Rights: Lynn Lewis, Maggie Schreiner, and Cynthia Tobar (Moderated by Stephanie Neel)
  • December 02, 2025: Survival Without Rent and Viva Loisaida!: film screening + Q&A with filmmakers Katie Heiserman and Elana Meyers (Moderated by Justin Mugits)
  • January 03, 2026: Fragile Dwelling: Margaret Morton's documentation of New York's homeless communities, a talk by Bonnie Yochelson

Special thanks to the following institutional archives: Centro, the Center for Puerto Rican Studies, Hunter College; the Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Columbia University; and Tamiment Library and Robert F. Wagner Archives, New York University Special Collections.
