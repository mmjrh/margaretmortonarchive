Bonnie Yochelson (she/her/hers) is an art historian and independent curator who has organized exhibitions and written books on many contemporary and historically significant photographers, including Berenice Abbott, Alfred Stieglitz and Jacob Riis. Her most recent book is Too Good to Get Married: The Life and Photographs of Miss Alice Austen (Fordham University Press, 2025).

Stephanie Neel (she/her/hers) is an archivist based in New York City, working primarily with photography and performing arts collections. She has been the archivist at Mark Morris Dance Group in Brooklyn since 2017, and has worked with a number of performing arts companies and artist estates as a consultant for grant writing, project formation, digitization, and database advisement. Stephanie is the current president of Archivists Round Table of Metropolitan New York, and serves on the Archives Funding Coalition for Dance/USA.

Justin Han (he/him/his) is an archivist and artist based in Brooklyn. He received his BA in Archaeology and BFA in Painting from the Brown-RISD Dual Degree Program in 2022, and was formerly an archivist with the Mark Morris Dance Group. He has received fellowships and residencies from Vermont Studio Center, The Here and There Collective, The Sharpe-Walentas Studio Program, and Asia Art Archive in America.

Alexandra Arredondo (she/her/hers) is currently a graduate student at Pratt Institute studying Library and Information Science alongside receiving a certificate in Archives. Her work centers around audiovisual collections, primarily focusing on migrant narratives which include oral history and documentary work.
